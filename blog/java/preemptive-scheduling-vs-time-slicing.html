What is the difference between preemptive scheduling and time slicing?

Published: June 20, 2017

Preemptive scheduling: The highest priority task executes until it enters the waiting or dead states or a higher priority task comes into existence. Time slicing: A task executes for a predefined slice of time and then reenters the pool of ready tasks. The scheduler then determines which task should execute next, based on priority and other factors.
